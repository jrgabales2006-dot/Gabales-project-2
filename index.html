Welcome to KCC

A college education shaped by faith and possibility.

At Kabankalan Catholic College, education is about more than what you learn. It is also about the person you become, the relationships you build, and the good you bring to the world around you.

Here in the heart of Kabankalan, students are invited to find their strengths, pursue meaningful goals, and grow as thoughtful, compassionate members of their community.
