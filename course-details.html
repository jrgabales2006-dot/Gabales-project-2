Before you apply

Ask for details that match your goals.

This page is a guide to requesting program information, not an official course catalog. Offerings, course sequences, and requirements may change, so confirm them with the college for your intended academic year.

Program overview

Request the current description, learning outcomes, and the types of study or work the program prepares students for.

Entry requirements

Ask which qualifications, documents, or other steps are required for new applicants.

Program timeline

Confirm the program's schedule, application dates, and expected enrollment period.

Prepare your questions

  • Is this program accepting students for my intended school year?
  • What subjects or learning experiences are included?
  • Are there prerequisites or additional requirements?
  • What current fees and payment arrangements should I know about?
