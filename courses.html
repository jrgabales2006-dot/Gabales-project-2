Senior High School

KCC Senior High School has both Academic and Technical-Vocational-Livelihood tracks so students can follow the path that fits their interests and goals. Every track includes Catholic values formation and activities that develop the whole person.

Academic Track

Academic Strands

For students headed to college or university.

STEM — Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

For students who intend to pursue college degrees in the sciences, engineering, technology, mathematics, and related fields. Focuses on advanced mathematics, sciences, and research.

Key Subjects

  • Pre-Calculus
  • Basic Calculus
  • General Biology
  • General Chemistry
  • General Physics
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Engineering · Medicine · IT/Computer Science · Architecture · Research

ABM — Accountancy, Business, and Management

Designed for students who plan to pursue business-related courses in college such as business administration, accountancy, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

Key Subjects

  • Fundamentals of ABM
  • Business Mathematics
  • Business Finance
  • Organization & Management
  • Applied Economics
  • Principles of Marketing

College Paths

Accountancy · Business Administration · Entrepreneurship · Marketing · Economics

HUMSS — Humanities and Social Sciences

For students who wish to take up journalism, communication arts, liberal arts, education, and other social science programs in college.

Key Subjects

  • Creative Writing
  • Creative Nonfiction
  • Introduction to World Religions
  • Trends, Networks, & Critical Thinking
  • Philippine Politics & Governance
  • Community Engagement

College Paths

Education · Law · Psychology · Mass Communication · Public Administration

GAS — General Academic Strand

A flexible strand for students who are still undecided on their college course. It covers a broad range of subjects from different academic tracks.

Key Subjects

  • Humanities
  • Social Sciences
  • Applied Economics
  • Organization & Management
  • Electives from other strands
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Any college program · Liberal Arts · Education · Business · Social Sciences

College of

Management, Accountancy and Technology

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration

The BSBA program prepares students for careers in business and management. Students develop competencies in financial analysis, marketing strategy, organizational management, and entrepreneurship.

Majors / Specializations

  • Financial Management
  • Marketing Management

Career Opportunities

Financial Analyst · Marketing Manager · HR Specialist · Entrepreneur · Business Consultant

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

The BSIT program develops future-ready IT professionals with skills in software development, networking, database management, and emerging technologies. Students gain hands-on experience in building real-world applications.

Career Opportunities

Software Developer · Network Administrator · Database Administrator · IT Consultant · Web Developer

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy

The BSA program develops competent and ethical accountants prepared for the CPA licensure examination. Students gain expertise in financial accounting, auditing, taxation, and management advisory services.

Career Opportunities

Certified Public Accountant · External Auditor · Tax Consultant · Financial Controller · Government Auditor

Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information System

The BSAIS program integrates accounting knowledge with information technology skills. Students learn to design, implement, and manage accounting information systems for modern business environments.

Career Opportunities

IT Auditor · Systems Analyst · ERP Consultant · Database Administrator · Financial Systems Manager

Bachelor of Science in Management Accounting

The BSMA program focuses on strategic financial management and decision-making. Students develop skills in cost analysis, budgeting, performance evaluation, and strategic planning for organizations.

Career Opportunities

Management Accountant · Cost Analyst · Budget Manager · Financial Planner · Business Consultant

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management

The BSTM program prepares students for careers in the dynamic tourism and hospitality industry. Students learn tourism planning, event management, and sustainable tourism development.

Career Opportunities

Tour Operations Manager · Hotel Manager · Travel Consultant · Event Coordinator · Tourism Officer

College of

Education, Arts and Sciences

Bachelor of Elementary Education

The BEEd program prepares dedicated and competent educators for elementary education. The curriculum provides strong pedagogical foundations and content knowledge for effective teaching of young learners.

Majors / Specializations

  • General Education

Career Opportunities

Elementary School Teacher · Education Specialist · Curriculum Developer · School Administrator

Bachelor of Secondary Education

The BSEd program trains future secondary school teachers with specializations in various fields of study. Graduates are equipped with advanced subject matter knowledge and teaching methodologies.

Majors / Specializations

  • English
  • Filipino
  • Values Education
  • Science
  • Mathematics

Career Opportunities

High School Teacher · Subject Coordinator · Education Researcher · Guidance Counselor

Bachelor of Early Childhood Education

The BECEd program prepares educators specializing in early childhood development and education. Students learn to create meaningful learning experiences that nurture curiosity, confidence, and a love for learning in young children.

Career Opportunities

Preschool Teacher · Early Childhood Specialist · Child Development Worker · Daycare Center Manager

Bachelor of Physical Education

The BPEd program develops competent physical education teachers and sports professionals. Students gain knowledge in exercise science, sports coaching, and health-related fitness instruction.

Career Opportunities

PE Teacher · Sports Coach · Fitness Instructor · Athletic Director · Sports Development Officer

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy

The AB Philosophy program develops critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and ethical reflection. Students explore fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, and the human condition.

Career Opportunities

Philosophy Professor · Seminary Studies · Ethics Consultant · Writer/Journalist · Law School Preparation

Bachelor of Science in Psychology

The BS Psychology program provides a comprehensive understanding of human behavior, mental processes, and psychological well-being. Students develop skills in counseling, assessment, and research.

Career Opportunities

Psychologist · Guidance Counselor · Human Resource Officer · Behavioral Therapist · Research Analyst
