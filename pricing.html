Getting started

Know what to ask before you enroll.

School fees can depend on your program, year level, and the current academic year. Ask the college office for an official, itemized fee schedule so you can plan with up-to-date information.

Request a current breakdown

Ask which tuition and other school fees apply to your intended program and year level.

Confirm payment dates

Check the current enrollment deadlines, payment schedule, and accepted payment methods with the college.

Ask about assistance

Contact the school to ask whether financial assistance or other support options are currently available.

Get official fee information.

For accurate amounts and enrollment requirements, reach out to the college office. Let them know which program and school year you are asking about.

Contact the college
This page does not publish tuition amounts. Please confirm all costs and payment arrangements with Kabankalan Catholic College before making enrollment decisions.
Helpful questions

Before you contact the college

What details should I provide?

Share the program you are interested in, your intended year level, and the academic year you plan to attend.

Can I rely on an older fee schedule?

Fees and enrollment requirements may change. Always request the latest official information from the college.

Where can I ask about admissions?

Use the admissions and visit information on our homepage to find KCC and plan your inquiry.

How do I visit the campus?

Check the college location and confirm office hours with the school before traveling.
